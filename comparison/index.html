⚠️ Crypto is high-risk — prices are extremely volatile and you can lose your entire investment. There is generally no investor-compensation scheme — unlike the brokers above, crypto holdings and cash at these exchanges are not covered by any bank deposit guarantee scheme. Only ever use MiCA-licensed platforms, and never invest more than you can afford to lose.
