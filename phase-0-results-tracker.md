## The one number that decides everything

**Signup rate = waitlist signups ÷ unique visitors.**

| Result | Reading | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **≥ 11%** | Strong — real demand | ✅ Green-light Phase 1 fully + trigger the paid legal consult |
| **7–11%** | Promising | 🟡 Iterate copy/positioning, run a second push before committing |
| **5–7%** | Weak but alive | 🟠 Something's off — diagnose from comments before more work |
| **< 5%** | Not landing | 🔴 Stop and rethink offer/audience before building anything |

(Benchmarks: ~11% is the waitlist median; general landing pages ~6.6%.)

## Decision tree (what each outcome triggers)

**🟢 ≥11% overall →** Demand validated.
- Move Phase 1 from draft to real: finalize lessons, commission the visuals, book the fixed-fee legal consult (take `compliance-one-pager.md`'s 6 questions).
- Note which pillar converted better — it shapes launch messaging and which affiliate stream to prioritize.

**🟡 7–11% →** Close. Don't build yet; sharpen first.
- Rewrite the hero around the higher-converting pillar/objection, re-test on 2–3 fresh channels.
- If it clears 11% on the second push → treat as green.

**🟠 5–7% →** Diagnose before investing.
- Is it the *offer* (people don't want it) or the *funnel* (wrong audience / weak copy / bad channel)? The per-channel table tells you: if one channel converted well and others didn't, it's targeting, not the idea.
- Fix the most likely cause, re-test once.

**🔴 <5% →** Stop and rethink.
- The comments will say why. Common pivots: narrower audience, a sharper single pillar (crypto-only or investing-only), or a different value prop (e.g. "avoid crypto scams" if that's what resonated).
- Do **not** proceed to build. Re-validate the new angle with another cheap landing test.

**Split signal (one pillar strong, other weak) →**
- Consider leading with the winner and making the other secondary. E.g. if crypto converts 15% and investing 4%, the honest-crypto angle may be the real wedge — but weigh the higher MiCA weighting that implies (see `compliance-one-pager.md`).

## Pre-registered guardrails (decide now, so you don't move goalposts later)

- **Minimum sample before deciding:** ~300 visitors. Fewer than that = keep driving traffic, don't over-read early noise.
- **Don't rescue a weak result with paid ads.** Ads test whether you can *rent* attention, not whether the idea is good. Organic first.
- **One iteration limit at each tier.** If two honest attempts can't clear the bar, respect the signal rather than endlessly tweaking.
- **Founding-intent check (bonus signal):** the waitlist form's "would you pay?" question logs `yes` / `maybe` / `no` per signup automatically — pull the % who said yes (or yes+maybe) from Formspree. Willingness-to-pay is worth more than raw signups.
