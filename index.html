Why this exists

Investing feels intimidating. Crypto feels like a minefield.

Every app wants you to deposit money now. The stocks apps ignore crypto; the crypto apps are full of hype and people trying to sell you something. Almost none of them slow down to honestly teach you what you’re actually doing.

This is the opposite: learn first, invest second, across both worlds. Understand the handful of ideas that matter, get a straight, no-hype take on crypto including the risks, decide what fits you, and take the first step feeling calm instead of anxious.

It’s built around how this works in Europe specifically: the real tools, the protections, the fees, and only regulated, licensed platforms. Not a US app with the wrong currency.

What we’ll never do

  • No price predictions or “this coin is going to X”
  • No coin picks or “buy this” recommendations, ever
  • No leveraged trading tutorials or margin/futures content
  • No paid coin promotions or influencer partnerships
  • No “guaranteed returns” language, anywhere

What we do instead

  • Teach how investing and crypto actually work, in plain language
  • A quiz that sorts you into a type, not a personal recommendation
  • Straight talk on risk, fees, and what’s protected, stocks and crypto alike
  • Only regulated EU brokers and MiCA-licensed exchanges
  • Self-custody and scam-spotting, so the obvious traps don’t get you
